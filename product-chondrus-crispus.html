What is in it

Three things. Chondrus crispus from the cold Atlantic, wild harvested. Water. Omega DHA oil, the same oil we sell in a bottle on its own.

Nothing else is added: no second alga, no preservative, no thickener, no sweetener. If you want the shore rather than the one plant, that is Northern Soul.

How to take it

One tablespoon each morning, on an empty stomach. Straight off the spoon and chased with water, or stirred into a warm tea with raw honey until it dissolves, or blended into a smoothie. It goes into soup as well, blended in at the end.

If you miss a morning, do not take two the next day. Seaweed carries iodine, and there is an upper limit on how much of that you want in a day.

On skin and hair

Customers use this jar as a face mask or a hair mask: on for up to an hour, then rinsed off with water only, and patted dry rather than rubbed. That is a description of what people do with it, not a claim about what it does.

It is sold as a food and it is not registered as a cosmetic, so read it as a food you have decided to put on your face. Try a little on the inside of your wrist first, and keep it out of your eyes.

Storage

Keep it in the fridge and use it within 28 days of opening. It freezes well if you will not get through it in time. Use a clean, dry spoon and put the lid back on after each serving.

Why gel goes watery, and what to do about it, is set out in the sea moss article.

Who should not take it

Anyone with an overactive thyroid should leave it alone. If you take thyroid medication, or you are pregnant or nursing, ask your GP or pharmacist before you start, and keep it away from children.

We do not print a per-spoon iodine figure for the gel, because it varies with the harvest. If you need to count iodine, Seaking prints 75 µg per capsule.

The routine

How to take it

Step one

Start with a clean, dry spoon. A used one carries bacteria into the jar and shortens its life.

Step two

Take one tablespoon first thing, before you have eaten.

Step three

Chase it with a glass of water. If you would rather not take it neat, stir it into tea with raw honey until it dissolves, or blend it into a smoothie.

Step four

Put the lid on and put it back in the fridge. It keeps for 28 days once opened. Freeze half if that is longer than you need.

What is on the inside

One alga, water, one oil

Three things, and this is all three of them. The whole declared list fits on one line.

Red · Rhodophyta

Chondrus crispusIrish sea moss

Picked by hand off cold Atlantic rocks at low tide. Flat, forking fronds that dry dark. This is the plant the old herbals call carrageen, and it is the only alga in this jar.

Prepared with

WaterAnd nothing else

The seaweed is soaked and blended with water. There is no preservative, no thickener and no sweetener, which is why the jar has 28 days in it rather than a year.

Added

Omega DHA OilThe bottle we sell at £19.99

An oil blended at six parts omega 3 to one part omega 6. The listing does not print how much of it goes into a jar, so neither do we. A measured serving is a tablespoon of the bottle, not a spoonful of this.

From Reiss

Why one alga and not four

Northern Soul is the jar with the rest of the shore in it. This is the other one. Cold Atlantic Chondrus crispus, water, and the oil. I keep making it because a fair number of people want one plant and one plant only, either because they are new to seaweed and want to know exactly what they have taken, or because they are putting it on their face and would rather not put four algae there.

The oil is in it and I will not pretend to you about why. The old listing gives a reason I am not allowed to repeat, and I am not going to write a new reason to fill the gap. What I can tell you is that the oil is Omega DHA, that we sell it in a bottle at £19.99, and that the amount in a jar of gel is not printed anywhere, so I do not print one.
