What it is

Shungite is a black rock with a high carbon content. The commercial stone is quarried in Karelia, in north west Russia, and takes its name from the village of Shunga. That is a fact about the mineral, not about this piece: the listing does not say where this one came from.

Elite Noble is a trade word for the shiny, high carbon sort. No assay comes with it, so this page prints no carbon figure of its own.

How many you get

One. The name is plural and the listing says one piece of about 10 g, so one is what this page promises.

If a second turns up in the envelope, that is the shop being generous rather than the listing being kept.

What this page does not say

The listing this piece came from makes a number of statements about what it does and where to put it, several of them about water. None of them are repeated here, and none of them are made here in different words either.

Water Stones is the name printed on the listing. On this page it is a name and nothing else.
