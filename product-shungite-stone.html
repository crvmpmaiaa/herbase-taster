What it is

Shungite is a black rock with a high carbon content. The commercial stone is quarried in Karelia, in north west Russia, and it takes its name from the village of Shunga. That is a fact about the mineral and not about this piece: the listing does not say where this one came out of the ground.

Elite Noble is a trade word for the shiny, high carbon sort. It arrives with no assay, so this page prints no carbon figure of its own.

What is printed

A weight, about 20 to 30 g, and a price. That is the whole of the specification, and everything else on the sheet below says not printed.

No dimensions, no finish, no origin for this piece, no certificate. If any of that gets printed it goes on the sheet, and until then the rows stay empty.

What this page does not say

The listing this stone came from makes a number of statements about what it does. None of them are repeated here, and none of them are made here in different words either.

It is a rock. We sell it as a rock, and there is nothing you have to do with it.

Why the page says so little

I stock shungite because people come into the shop and ask for it by name. What I will not do is print the reason they ask for it, because the reason is a claim and I cannot stand a claim up with a weight and a price.
