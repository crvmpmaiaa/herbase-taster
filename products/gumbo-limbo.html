Gumbo limbo capsules made from Bursera simaruba, a fast-growing tree of the Caribbean and Central America known for its red, peeling bark. The listing does not state a capsule count, a weight, or which part of the tree fills the capsule, so a longer article and a set of questions for Reiss sit alongside this shorter entry.

Bursera simaruba is a large tree native to the Caribbean, Central America and southern Florida, easy to spot by its thin, copper-red bark that peels in papery sheets, which has earned it the nickname tourist tree locally, after sunburnt visitors. It grows fast and is often used as a living fence post, since a cut branch will root and grow where it is planted.

The listing does not say which part of the tree goes into this capsule, nor how many capsules are in a pack or what each one weighs. Those are the three questions we have put to Reiss before this page can carry a full specification.
