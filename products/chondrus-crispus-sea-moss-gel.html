A plain Chondrus crispus gel, the cold-water Atlantic species also called Irish moss or carrageen, prepared with water and Omega DHA oil. Sold in a glass jar.

This is the plain version of the shop's cold-water sea moss: Chondrus crispus on its own, not blended with the other algae in Northern Soul. It is the same species that carries the name Irish moss and carrageen in the old herbals, and it is a different plant to the warm-water Eucheuma cottonii sold here as Purple Mwani.

The gel is prepared with water and Omega DHA oil, and nothing else is added. There is no printed per-spoon iodine figure, so no iodine claim is made here. Seaking, the capsule formula, prints a measured 75 µg per capsule.

Take one tablespoon daily, preferably in the morning on an empty stomach, either raw by the spoon and chased with water, stirred into tea with raw honey, blended into a smoothie, or worked through soup.

Who should not take this

Safety information is the one thing a shop is always allowed to tell you, so we tell you all of it.

  • Sea moss contains natural iodine, which varies from harvest to harvest. If you take thyroid medication, have a thyroid condition, or are pregnant or breastfeeding, ask your GP or pharmacist before taking this daily.
