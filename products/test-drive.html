Test Drive is a fifty capsule formula with all nine of its ingredients named on the listing, more completely specified than most of the range, though three of those names are broad enough (a genus, an unspecified species) to need confirming before we can print a full binomial for each.

Tribulus, <i>Tribulus terrestris</i>, is a low, spiny-fruited plant found across warm climates worldwide including southern Europe; tongkat ali, <i>Eurycoma longifolia</i>, is a slow-growing understorey tree from the rainforests of Southeast Asia, valued for its root. Epimedium is named only by genus on the listing, without a species, though the common commercial species used in supplements include <i>Epimedium grandiflorum</i>, <i>Epimedium sagittatum</i> and several others, so we've queried which one this is.

Fadogia, <i>Fadogia agrestis</i>, is a shrub from the coffee family native to West Africa, with a much shorter history in Western supplements than most of the herbs on this page. Sarsaparilla and damiana appear here as they do elsewhere on the site, <i>Smilax officinalis</i> and <i>Turnera diffusa</i> respectively. Pine pollen is named only as 'pine' without a species, and ginseng is named only as 'ginseng' without saying which species, so both need confirming too.

The ninth ingredient, piperine, isn't a whole herb but an alkaloid compound extracted from black pepper, <i>Piper nigrum</i>. The pack is fifty capsules at 800mg each.

How it is taken

Two capsules a day with breakfast, or one to two hours before exercise, with at least a litre of water.

Before you take it

Who should not take this

Safety information is the one thing a shop is always allowed to tell you, so we tell you all of it.

  • Not for use in pregnancy or while breastfeeding.
  • Formulated for men; the listing gives no guidance for anyone else.
  • Speak to your GP before taking this if you're on nitrates, blood pressure medication, heart medication or any other prescribed drugs. The listing states this formula can affect how the body processes other medicines, which can change how strong or how toxic those medicines are.
  • If you have a diagnosed medical condition, speak to your GP before taking this rather than treating it yourself.

If you take prescribed medication, are pregnant or breastfeeding, or are under the care of a doctor for anything at all, speak to them before starting a supplement. This is a food shop and not a medical service.
