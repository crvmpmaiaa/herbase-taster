Centella asiatica, a low, creeping herb of the carrot family, found right across South and South East Asia. No capsule count printed, no weight, no stated part of the plant. Open questions, all three, for Reiss.

Centella asiatica is a small creeping perennial of the carrot family, fan-shaped leaves on long thin stalks, tiny pinkish flowers hidden close to the ground. It grows in wet, marshy ground across South and South East Asia, eaten fresh as a salad leaf in Sri Lanka and Indonesia, and dried for herbal use besides.

Leaves and stems together, the aerial parts, are what most commonly gets dried for a capsule. Whether that is actually what fills this pack, the listing never confirms. So we have asked.

How it is taken
