Gotu kola capsules made from Centella asiatica, a low, creeping herb of the carrot family found across South and South East Asia. The listing does not print a capsule count, a weight, or which part of the plant is used, so those are open questions for Reiss.

Centella asiatica is a small creeping perennial in the carrot family, with fan-shaped leaves on long thin stalks and tiny pinkish flowers hidden close to the ground. It grows in wet, marshy ground across South and South East Asia, and is eaten as a fresh salad leaf in Sri Lanka and Indonesia as well as dried for herbal use.

The leaves and stems, known together as the aerial parts, are the part most commonly dried for capsules, though the listing does not confirm this is what fills the pack, so we have asked.
