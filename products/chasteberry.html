Vitex agnus-castus, a Mediterranean shrub of the mint family, its dried berry taken into fifty vegan capsules to a pack.

Vitex agnus-castus is a deciduous shrub native to the Mediterranean and central Asia, slender grey-green leaves, spikes of small violet flowers by late summer. The berry ripens dark and peppery, and that pepper is exactly where the old country name comes from: chaste tree, and on the trade shelf, monk's pepper.

The dried berry is what goes into the capsule. No weight per capsule is printed, no extraction ratio either, so how concentrated the fill actually is against the raw berry, we cannot say.

Fifty to a pack. Two a day, first thing, with water, is what the label recommends.

How it is taken

Two capsules a day, first thing in the morning, before breakfast, with at least 500ml water.

Fifty capsules. Two a day makes twenty five days, which is the supply printed on the label, then five days off.

Who should not take this

Safety information is the one thing a shop is always allowed to tell you, so we tell you all of it.
