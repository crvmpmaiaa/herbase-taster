Decolonise is a fifty capsule formula built on nine named ingredients: seven herbs, psyllium husk and activated charcoal from coconut shells. The dose and timing printed on the pack are unusually specific, capped at two capsules a day and no more than once every four months, and that instruction matters enough to repeat here in full.

Pau d'arco, <i>Tabebuia impetiginosa</i>, is the bark of a Central and South American flowering tree, and bitter melon, <i>Momordica charantia</i>, a warty fruit from a climbing Asian vine, both appear here as they do in Detox Redox. Anamu, <i>Petiveria alliacea</i>, is a leafy Caribbean and South American shrub the shop also sells on its own as a single-herb capsule, sometimes called guinea hen weed for its garlicky smell. Clove, <i>Syzygium aromaticum</i>, is the dried flower bud of a tropical evergreen tree native to Indonesia, familiar from any spice rack.

Wormseed, <i>Dysphania ambrosioides</i> (older texts call it <i>Chenopodium ambrosioides</i>), is a strong-smelling annual native to Central America and long used across Latin American herbal tradition; wormwood, <i>Artemisia absinthium</i>, is the grey-leaved European perennial best known for flavouring vermouth and absinthe. Black pepper, <i>Piper nigrum</i>, is the dried, ground fruit of a tropical vine, included here in whole or ground form rather than as an isolated compound. Psyllium husk, from <i>Plantago ovata</i>, is the fibrous seed coat of a small plantain grown mainly in India, and the ninth ingredient is activated charcoal made from organic coconut shells, <i>Cocos nucifera</i>.

The pack is fifty capsules at 800mg each, printed as a twenty five day supply at the maximum dose of two a day. The shop's own instructions cap this formula at once every four months, which is the single most important thing printed on the label and belongs here in full rather than being softened into vague advice.

How it is taken

Take as directed on the pack with 500ml of spring water. Do not exceed two capsules a day. Use once at a maximum every four months.

Before you take it

Who should not take this

Safety information is the one thing a shop is always allowed to tell you, so we tell you all of it.

  • Not for use in pregnancy or while breastfeeding.
  • Do not exceed two capsules a day.
  • Use once at a maximum every four months, as printed on the pack. This isn't a formula for daily or ongoing use.
  • Speak to your GP before taking this if you're on any prescribed medication.
  • If you have a diagnosed medical condition, speak to your GP before taking this rather than treating it yourself.

If you take prescribed medication, are pregnant or breastfeeding, or are under the care of a doctor for anything at all, speak to them before starting a supplement. This is a food shop and not a medical service.
