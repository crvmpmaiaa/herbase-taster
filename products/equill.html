Equill is a fifty capsule formula built on three named herbs, black cohosh, red clover and wild yam, alongside a 'Proprietary Herbal Blend' the shop lists as its own ingredient without saying what's in it. The listing's own dosing instructions disagree with each other, so we've flagged that rather than guess which one is right.

Black cohosh is <i>Actaea racemosa</i> (older sources call it <i>Cimicifuga racemosa</i>), a tall woodland perennial native to eastern North America, in the buttercup family, with long white flower spikes that give it its other common name, black snakeroot. It's the root and rhizome that go into supplements. Red clover, <i>Trifolium pratense</i>, is the familiar pink-flowered clover found across British hedgerows and pasture as well as being cultivated commercially; the flowering tops are the part used.

Wild yam, <i>Dioscorea villosa</i>, is a twining vine native to eastern North America and Central America, grown for its tuberous root. Beyond these three, the listing lists a 'Proprietary Herbal Blend' as a fourth named ingredient in its own ingredients table, without disclosing what it actually contains, which is a gap worth putting to Reiss directly rather than leaving unstated.

Each capsule is printed at 750mg, in a pack of fifty.

How it is taken

The listing gives two different doses. One version says two capsules a day, taken in the morning with water or food. Another says one capsule a day, increasing to two if needed, with a meal and 500ml of spring water. We've queried which is correct rather than pick one.

Fifty capsules, printed elsewhere on the listing as a thirty day supply, though neither of the two printed doses divides fifty capsules into exactly thirty days. Flagged as a query.
