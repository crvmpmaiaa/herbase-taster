Ginseng root capsules made from Panax ginseng, a slow-growing perennial in the ginseng family, native to East Asia. Fifty vegan capsules to a pack, 800mg each, with no fillers.

Panax ginseng is a slow-growing perennial in the Araliaceae family, native to the cool forests of Korea, north-east China and the Russian Far East. Wild plants take years to reach a harvestable size, which is why most ginseng sold today, including this pack, is cultivated rather than wild-dug.

The listing calls the product 'Ginseng (Siberian)' in its title, while the text underneath describes Panax ginseng throughout. Those are two different plants. Panax ginseng is the Asian ginseng associated with ginsenosides, and Siberian ginseng usually refers to Eleutherococcus senticosus, a related but distinct species with a different set of active compounds. We have asked Reiss which one is actually in the capsule.

Each capsule is 800mg, and the pack contains fifty, with no fillers.

Who should not take this
