Acacia bark capsules. Acacia is a large genus of well over a thousand species and the listing does not name which one this pack is made from, so the species is a confirmed open question rather than a guess. Fifty vegan capsules to a pack.

Acacia is one of the largest tree genera in the world, with species native to Africa, Arabia, southern Asia and Australia. Several are grown commercially for their bark and resin. Acacia senegal and Acacia seyal are the main sources of gum arabic, the hardened resin that seeps from cuts in the bark and is used as a food thickener and in traditional medicine across North Africa and the Middle East.

The listing describes the tree only as 'the ancient Acacia tree native to the Indus Kush region, stretching from North Africa through Arabia into India' and mentions gum arabic, which points toward Acacia senegal or a related gum-producing species, but no binomial is given anywhere on the listing. Because 'acacia bark' can mean the bark of any of dozens of commercially traded species, each with a different chemical profile, we have logged this as a query rather than picked one.
