A red Chondrus crispus sea moss gel from the cold Atlantic, blended with baobab, elderberry and hibiscus. Sold in a 300 ml or 500 ml glass jar.

Nu Heru starts from the same base as the rest of the sea moss shelf: Chondrus crispus, the red seaweed also called Irish moss or carrageen, which grows in cold Atlantic water rather than the warm farmed water some sea moss comes from. To that base the jar adds baobab, elderberry and hibiscus, three plants with a long trade history of their own and a dark red colour that carries through into the gel.

There is no printed iodine figure on this jar, the way there is on Seaking's capsule label, so nothing is claimed about iodine here. Sea moss carries iodine and the amount moves with the harvest, which is exactly why a capsule with a fixed dose is the only format on this site allowed to put a number next to it.

Take it by the spoon, stirred into a smoothie or juice, or into a warm tea with raw honey. Nothing else is added beyond the four named ingredients and the water the gel is prepared with.

How it is taken

One to two tablespoons daily, neat, in a smoothie or juice, or in tea with raw honey.

At one tablespoon a day, roughly three weeks from the 300 ml jar and a month from the 500 ml. At two tablespoons a day, about half that.

Before you take it

Who should not take this

Safety information is the one thing a shop is always allowed to tell you, so we tell you all of it.

  • Do not use this product if you have low blood pressure or take medication to lower blood pressure.
  • Sea moss contains natural iodine, which varies from harvest to harvest. If you take thyroid medication, have a thyroid condition, or are pregnant or breastfeeding, ask your GP or pharmacist before taking this daily.
  • Food supplements should not be used as a substitute for a varied and balanced diet and a healthy lifestyle. Keep out of reach of young children.
