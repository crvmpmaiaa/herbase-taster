Inonotus obliquus - sold as Chaga, 750 mg of extract to a capsule, described as free from fillers or additives. The listing doesn't print a capsule count.

Inonotus obliquus grows on living birch trees in cold climates right across the northern hemisphere, forming a hard, black, cracked mass on the trunk that looks more like burnt charcoal than a mushroom. That mass, properly called a conk or a sclerotium, is the part harvested and used, not a cap and gills at all.

Herbase's capsules list 750 mg of pure Chaga extract to a capsule, in a vegan formulation free from fillers or additives. As with the other mushroom pages in this batch, what isn't stated is whether that 750 mg is dried whole material or a concentrated extract at a given ratio, whether it's the conk itself or mycelium grown on grain, and what the beta-glucan content actually is. The pack doesn't state how many capsules it holds either.

How it is taken

Two capsules daily with breakfast, and at least 500 ml of spring water.
