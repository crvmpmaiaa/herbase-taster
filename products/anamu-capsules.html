Petiveria alliacea, sold as Anamu, 50 vegan capsules with no fillers, binders or artificial ingredients.

Petiveria alliacea is a shrub native to the Caribbean and South America, also known as guinea hen weed, mucura or tipi. It grows wild across the region and is a familiar plant in Caribbean and South American herbal traditions, most easily identified by the strong sulphurous, garlic-like smell that comes off its crushed leaves and root, a smell that carries through into the dried and encapsulated material.

That smell comes from sulphur compounds in the plant, including dibenzyl trisulphide, one of the compounds Anamu is best known for chemically. Herbase's capsules are 100% Petiveria alliacea, harvested from organically grown sources in the Caribbean and South America, with no fillers, binders or artificial ingredients added at the encapsulation stage.

Each pack is 50 vegan capsules. The listing's own guidance on how long that lasts doesn't agree with itself, at one capsule a day it calls fifty capsules a forty day supply, which is a discrepancy we've flagged rather than repeated here.

How it is taken

One to two capsules a day with a meal and 500 ml of spring water.
