A blend of four mushrooms, lion's mane, cordyceps, chaga and reishi, each described as 100% pure, in 50 vegan capsules at 800 mg. The capsule shell is hydroxypropyl methylcellulose.

Mycrodose combines four mushroom species in one capsule: Hericium erinaceus (lion's mane), Cordyceps militaris (cordyceps), Inonotus obliquus (chaga) and Ganoderma lucidum (reishi). Each is listed on the label as 100% pure, and the capsule shell itself is named as hydroxypropyl methylcellulose, a plant-derived vegan capsule material.

The pack is 50 capsules at 800 mg each. What isn't stated is how that 800 mg is divided between the four mushrooms, so we can't say how much of any single species is actually in a capsule. As with the single-species mushroom pages in this batch, the listing also doesn't say whether each mushroom is fruiting body or mycelium grown on grain, what extraction method is used, or what the beta-glucan content is, for the blend as a whole or for any of the four species individually.

How it is taken

Two capsules daily with a meal and at least 500 ml of spring water.

Fifty capsules. At two a day that's 25 days.
