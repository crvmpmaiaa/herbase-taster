Neptune is a fifty capsule formula built around four named herbs, soursop, chamomile, valerian and blue vervain, plus an undisclosed 'proprietary blend' the listing doesn't itemise. It's taken as a single capsule dose in the evening, on a twenty day a month pattern printed on the original listing.

Soursop is <i>Annona muricata</i>, a broad-leafed tree from the Annonaceae family grown across the Caribbean and tropical South America for its spiny green fruit; formulas like this one generally use the leaf rather than the fruit pulp, though the listing doesn't say which part went into this capsule. Chamomile is one of the best known plants in Western herbalism, a small daisy-flowered annual, though 'chamomile' covers two different genera sold under the same name, German chamomile and Roman chamomile, and the listing doesn't specify which one Neptune uses.

Valerian, <i>Valeriana officinalis</i>, is a tall perennial with clusters of small pale pink flowers, native across Europe and parts of Asia; it's the root that goes into supplements, and it has a strong, distinctive smell that surprises people who've only met it as a capsule. Blue vervain, <i>Verbena hastata</i>, is a North American native with narrow spikes of small purple-blue flowers, traditionally gathered from wet meadows and riverbanks. Beyond these four, the listing names a 'proprietary blend' without saying what's in it, so we can't specify the rest of the capsule.

The pack is fifty large vegan capsules, taken one at a time in the evening rather than the two-a-day pattern used across most of this range.

How it is taken

Two capsules in the evening with 500ml of water, printed as best taken around 8pm. The listing suggests using it for twenty days a month.

Fifty capsules. At two a day that's twenty five days; used on the printed twenty-day-a-month pattern, one pack covers a little more than a calendar month.

Who should not take this

Safety information is the one thing a shop is always allowed to tell you, so we tell you all of it.

  • Not for use in pregnancy or while breastfeeding.
  • Speak to your GP before taking this if you're on prescribed medication.
  • If you have a diagnosed medical condition, speak to your GP before taking this rather than treating it yourself.
