Respire is a forty capsule formula, built around one named herb, amor seco, with the rest of the blend held back as a 'proprietary blend' the listing never itemises. Even the one herb it names needs a query. 'Amor seco' is a common name for more than one plant across South American herbalism, and the listing never says which.

'Amor seco' is Spanish for 'dry love', and the name covers several different plants across Central and South American herbal tradition depending on the region, which makes a single Latin binomial hard to pin down without more from the shop. Beyond that one named herb the listing falls back to 'proprietary blend' for the rest of the formula, and there's nothing further here we can describe with any confidence.

The pack is forty vegan capsules with no fillers, taken two at a time with water.

How it is taken

Two capsules a day, with 500ml of spring water, best taken at lunchtime as printed.
