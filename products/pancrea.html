Pancrea is sold as a vegan capsule formula. The live listing calls it a blend of traditional herbs without naming a single one of them, so this page can only publish what the shop actually prints: the form, the dose and the safety information. The herb list itself is the first thing we need from Reiss before this page can grow past a stub.

The old Pancrea listing opened with several lines of unauthorised claims naming an internal organ and its function, phrased in a way that would need this product to be licensed as a medicine before any of it could be repeated. None of that survives here, whatever herbs turn out to be inside the capsule.

What we cannot do is guess at a replacement. Most of the shop's other formulas name their herbs even where the surrounding claims have to go, which lets us write a proper botanical page instead of a claims page. Pancrea doesn't do that: the body copy names no herbs at all, only 'traditional herbs' and 'wildcrafted botanicals'. The listing's own tags mention chromium and vanadium, but neither mineral is stated as an ingredient in the body copy, so neither is included in the specification below. Until Reiss confirms what is actually in the jar, this page stays this short on purpose rather than filling the gap with a plausible-sounding herb list.

Before you take it

Who should not take this

Safety information is the one thing a shop is always allowed to tell you, so we tell you all of it.

  • Not for use in pregnancy or while breastfeeding.
  • Speak to your GP before taking this if you're on any prescribed medication: the formula's ingredients aren't fully published, so a possible interaction can't be ruled out.
  • If you have a diagnosed medical condition, speak to your GP before taking this rather than treating it yourself.
  • Stop taking it and see a doctor if you notice any reaction you weren't expecting.

If you take prescribed medication, are pregnant or breastfeeding, or are under the care of a doctor for anything at all, speak to them before starting a supplement. This is a food shop and not a medical service.
