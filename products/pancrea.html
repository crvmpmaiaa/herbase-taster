Pancrea is sold as a vegan capsule formula, and the live listing calls it a blend of traditional herbs without naming a single one of them. So this page carries only what the shop actually prints, the form, the dose and the safety information, and stops there. The herb list is the first thing we need from Reiss before this page can grow into anything more.

The old Pancrea listing opened on several lines about an internal organ and what it does, claims that would need this product licensed as a medicine before a word of them could be repeated. None of it survives here, whatever herbs the capsule turns out to hold.

What we can't do is guess a replacement. Most of the shop's other formulas name their herbs even where the claims around them have to go, which lets us write a proper botanical page instead of a claims page. Pancrea doesn't do that, the body copy names no herbs at all, only 'traditional herbs' and 'wildcrafted botanicals'. The listing's own tags mention chromium and vanadium, but neither mineral is stated as an ingredient in the body copy, so neither goes into the specification below. Until Reiss confirms what's actually growing in the jar, this page stays this short on purpose, not padded out with a plausible-sounding herb list.

Before you take it

Who should not take this

Safety information is the one thing a shop is always allowed to tell you, so we tell you all of it.

  • Not for use in pregnancy or while breastfeeding.
  • Speak to your GP before taking this if you're on any prescribed medication. The formula's ingredients aren't fully published, so a possible interaction can't be ruled out.
  • If you have a diagnosed medical condition, speak to your GP before taking this rather than treating it yourself.
  • Stop taking it and see a doctor if you notice any reaction you weren't expecting.

If you take prescribed medication, are pregnant or breastfeeding, or are under the care of a doctor for anything at all, speak to them before starting a supplement. This is a food shop and not a medical service.
