Mag Flux is a fifty capsule formula built on five fully named herbs: sarsaparilla, burdock, yellow dock, baobab and chickweed. It's one of the more completely specified formulas in this batch, with every ingredient named on the listing itself.

Sarsaparilla is given on the listing as <i>Smilax officinalis</i>, a climbing vine whose root has a long history in Central American herbal trade; burdock, <i>Arctium lappa</i>, is the familiar European biennial best known for the burred seed heads that stick to clothing, with the root used here. Yellow dock, <i>Rumex crispus</i>, is a common dock species found across hedgerows and waste ground in Britain, grown for its long taproot.

Baobab, <i>Adansonia digitata</i>, is the fruit of the African savannah tree, sold as a dried powder from the pulp inside its hard pod; chickweed, <i>Stellaria media</i>, is a low, sprawling annual found in gardens and disturbed ground across the UK, traditionally gathered as a fresh spring green as much as used dried. The listing also states the formula contains iron, zinc and B vitamins, though it doesn't print a quantity for any of them, so none of the associated authorised nutrient claims can be made on this page without that figure.

The pack is fifty capsules at 800mg each.

How it is taken

Two capsules with lunch or an afternoon meal, with 500ml of spring water.
