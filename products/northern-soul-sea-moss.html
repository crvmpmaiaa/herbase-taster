A Chondrus crispus gel from the cold Atlantic, wild harvested, carrying a blend of four ocean algae from three families. 300 ml or 500 ml, prepared with water only.

The base of the jar is Chondrus crispus, the cold-water red seaweed also called Irish moss or carrageen, picked by hand off Atlantic rock rather than grown in a warm farmed lagoon. Through it runs a blend of four ocean algae from three families, grouped by pigment: red, brown and green.

The gel carries no printed per-spoon iodine figure, because the amount moves with the harvest, so no iodine claim is made on this page. Seaking, the capsule version of this formula, prints a measured 75 µg of iodine per capsule if that number matters to you.

Take one tablespoon each morning, on an empty stomach, straight off the spoon and chased with water, or stirred into tea with raw honey, or blended into a smoothie. It is prepared with water and nothing else.

How it is taken

500 ml is about 33 tablespoons, a bit over a month. 300 ml is about 20 tablespoons, roughly three weeks.
