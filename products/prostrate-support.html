Prostrate is sold as vegan capsules, two a day. Like Pancrea, the listing describes 'a targeted blend of wildcrafted herbs' without naming a single one of them, so this page can only carry the form, the dose and the safety information the shop already prints.

The name is kept exactly as the shop spells it, Prostrate rather than the more familiar Prostate: that's the shop's own deliberate spelling and not a typo we've corrected.

What we can't do is describe the formula itself. The listing calls it 'a targeted blend of wildcrafted herbs traditionally used', without naming any of them, and stops there. That puts Prostrate alongside Pancrea and Nutropic as one of the three listings in this batch that name no ingredients at all, which is the single biggest gap on this page.

Before you take it

Who should not take this

Safety information is the one thing a shop is always allowed to tell you, so we tell you all of it.

  • Not intended for use in pregnancy or while breastfeeding.
  • Speak to your GP before taking this if you're on any prescribed medication: the formula's ingredients aren't published, so a possible interaction can't be ruled out.
  • If you have a diagnosed medical condition, speak to your GP before taking this rather than treating it yourself.

If you take prescribed medication, are pregnant or breastfeeding, or are under the care of a doctor for anything at all, speak to them before starting a supplement. This is a food shop and not a medical service.
