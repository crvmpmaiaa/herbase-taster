Astragalus capsules made from Astragalus membranaceus, a perennial legume long used in Chinese herbal medicine. Fifty vegan capsules to a pack, 800mg each, with no fillers or bulking agents.

Astragalus membranaceus is a perennial member of the pea family, growing to about a metre with feathery leaves and small pale yellow flowers. It is native to northern and eastern China, Mongolia and Korea, where it has been grown as a field crop for centuries.

The listing does not say which part of the plant this pack is made from. Astragalus root is the part almost always used commercially, and the trade name huang qi refers specifically to the dried root in Chinese medicine, but we have asked Reiss to confirm rather than assume.

Fifty capsules to a pack, 800mg each, with no fillers or bulking agents.
