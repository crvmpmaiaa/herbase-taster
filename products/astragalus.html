Astragalus membranaceus, a perennial of the pea family grown across China for centuries. Fifty vegan capsules to a pack, 800mg each. No fillers, no bulking agents.

Astragalus membranaceus is a perennial of the pea family, growing to about a metre, feathery leaves, small pale yellow flowers. Native to northern and eastern China, Mongolia and Korea, where it has stood as a field crop for centuries.

Which part of the plant fills this pack, the listing does not say. Root is almost always the part used commercially; huang qi, the trade name, refers specifically to the dried root in Chinese medicine. We have asked Reiss to confirm it rather than assume it.

Fifty to a pack, 800mg each. No fillers, no bulking agents.
