Chanca piedra capsules made from Phyllanthus niruri, a small annual herb of the Amazon basin and other tropical regions. Each capsule is 800mg, though the listing does not state how many capsules are in a pack.

Phyllanthus niruri is a small, upright annual herb, rarely taller than half a metre, with feathery rows of tiny leaflets along thin stems and seed-like flowers that sit directly under each leaf. It grows widely through the Amazon basin and other tropical and subtropical regions, often as a weed of disturbed and cultivated ground, and has a long history of use across South American herbal traditions.

Each capsule is 800mg of the extract, with no fillers or additives, though the listing does not say which part of the plant, or the whole plant, went into that extract, and does not state how many capsules make up a pack.

How it is taken

Two capsules in the morning with at least 500ml water, and two capsules in the evening with a meal and at least 500ml water.
