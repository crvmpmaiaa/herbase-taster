What is in the box

Three jars of Detox Redox at 40 large vegan capsules each, one jar of Northern Soul sea moss gel, and one copy of the book, written by Reiss Davies Ausar. The cards below go to each item's own sheet.

The listing says the book comes with the Konscious Konsumption nutrition guide. Nothing else is named as being in the box.

What the listing says about taking it

For the box, nothing. There is no schedule, no daily count and no order of items printed on this listing. It says the book carries the step by step instructions.

For the two jars separately: the gel's page prints one tablespoon each morning. Detox Redox prints no serving at all, which is on its sheet and on this one.

Storage

The gel goes in the fridge, used within 28 days of opening or frozen, with a clean dry spoon. The capsules do not want the fridge. Detox Redox prints no storage instruction of its own, so keep the lid on somewhere cool and dry, out of the sun.

Who should not take it

Anyone with an overactive thyroid should leave the gel alone, and if you take thyroid medication ask your GP or pharmacist before you start. Seaweed carries iodine.

Not in pregnancy or while breastfeeding. Not for children. If you take prescribed medicine of any kind, including a blood thinner or anything for diabetes, speak to your GP or pharmacist first. Detox Redox does not print a full ingredient list, and nobody can give you a caution for an ingredient nobody has printed.

Reiss's turn

Why the box cannot print a day rate

Three jars, a gel and a book, and the thing missing from the middle of it is a number. Detox Redox carries no serving on its own listing: no dose, no daily count, no course length. So when somebody at the counter asks me whether three jars is a month of anything, the honest answer is that I cannot work it out from what is printed, and neither can you.

The gel is the part I can be exact about. One tablespoon each morning, in the fridge, used inside 28 days of opening. Which of the two jars goes in this box is not printed either, and the two sizes are not the same price, so there is no sold separately total on the sheet above. I would rather leave that row empty than put a figure in it and hope nobody checks.

The book is where the listing sends you for the instructions, and that is where I would send you as well. If you want the serving before you buy rather than after, ring the counter and I will read you what the current label says.
