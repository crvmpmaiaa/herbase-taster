What is in the box

One 500 ml jar of the Northern Soul gel. One jar of Anamu, 50 large vegan capsules. One jar of Mag Flux, 50 large vegan capsules. One bottle of Omega DHA Oil.

The listing prints a one line ingredient note against each of the four. The full lists, and the gaps in them, are on each item's own sheet.

The schedule as printed on the listing

Morning: one tablespoon of the sea moss gel. Lunch or the afternoon: one or two Mag Flux capsules. Evening meal: one or two Anamu capsules. Any time in the day: one tablespoon of the Omega DHA Oil, with water or food.

At least 500 ml of spring water with the capsules, as printed. No course length is printed for the box.

How long the four last

They do not run out together. A 500 ml jar of gel is about 33 tablespoons. Two jars of 50 capsules are 25 to 50 days each, depending on whether you take one capsule or two.

The bottle is the odd one. This listing calls it a two month supply; the Omega DHA Oil listing calls it one month at a tablespoon a day. Both figures are printed by us, in different places, and they do not agree. We print both rather than pick.

Storage

The gel goes in the fridge, used within 28 days of opening or frozen, with a clean dry spoon. Capsules keep their lid on somewhere cool and dry, no fridge. The oil bottle prints no storage instruction of its own, so keep it closed and out of the sun.

Who should not take it

Not for children. Not in pregnancy or while breastfeeding without asking a midwife, GP or pharmacist first: that caution sits on three of the four sheets.

If you take warfarin or any other anticoagulant, or you have surgery booked, ask before you start. Anyone with an overactive thyroid should leave the sea moss alone. Yellow dock, in Mag Flux, is high in oxalate. The oil does not state its source, so if you avoid particular oils, ring the shop before you buy.

Delivery and collection

Sent out from 599 Smithdown Road, Liverpool, Tuesday to Sunday. A box with a gel in it goes out chilled. UK delivery is free over £50, or you can collect from the shop between 10 and 6, any day except Monday.

Reiss's turn

Four items, four different clocks

A box implies the things in it run together, and these four do not. The gel is about 33 tablespoons. The two capsule jars are 25 days at two a day, or 50 at one, and the listing prints one or two for both, so even those two do not agree with each other. Somebody working through this box will finish one jar a fortnight before another.

The bottle is worse, and it is our own fault. This listing calls it a two month supply. The bottle's own listing calls it one month. Nobody has printed a volume, so there is no measurement that settles it, and I am not going to quietly choose the flattering one. Both figures are in the sheet above with their sources.

There is no sold separately total on this page either, because the gel comes in two jars at two prices. I could pick the 500 ml price, add it up and print a saving. I would rather send you to the four pages, where every price is printed on its own, and let you do the addition with the sizes you would actually buy.
