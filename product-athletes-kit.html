What is in the box

One 500 ml jar of the Northern Soul gel. One jar of Mag Flux, one of Test Drive and one of Mycrodose, 50 capsules in each. One bottle of Omega DHA Oil. One copy of the book, which the listing describes as the guide to fitting the six together.

The six cards below go to each item's own sheet, where the ingredient lists and the gaps in them are set out.

The schedule as printed on the listing

Morning: one tablespoon of the sea moss gel on an empty stomach, then one tablespoon of the Omega DHA Oil and one or two Test Drive capsules, which the listing says to adjust for the intensity of the training. Afternoon: one or two Mag Flux capsules with at least 500 ml of water and a meal. Evening: two Mycrodose capsules with at least 500 ml of water and a meal.

That is the listing's own wording. Two of the jars print different servings on their own sheets, and both differences are on the specification sheet below.

If you compete

Mag Flux and Test Drive are both printed as proprietary blends. Test Drive names some of its plants and Mag Flux names none, so for either jar there is no full list of what is in a capsule.

If you are tested, that matters more than any figure on this page. Nobody at this shop can hand you a complete ingredient list for those two, so ask your governing body or your doctor before you take them, not after.

Who should not take it

Not for children. Not in pregnancy or while breastfeeding. The Test Drive listing states that jar is for men only; it gives no reason, and we are not going to invent one.

If you take nitrates, blood pressure medicine, heart medicine, an anticoagulant, insulin or anything else for blood sugar, or any other prescribed medicine, speak to your GP or pharmacist before you start. There are four mushrooms in the evening jar, so anyone who reacts to mushrooms should leave it alone, and pine pollen in Test Drive is a pollen. Anyone with an overactive thyroid should leave the sea moss alone.

Delivery and collection

Sent out from 599 Smithdown Road, Liverpool, Tuesday to Sunday. A box with a gel in it goes out chilled. UK delivery is free over £50, or you can collect from the shop between 10 and 6, any day except Monday.
