What is in it

Ganoderma lucidum, dried and milled into a vegan capsule. The listing prints it as 100 per cent reishi and names nothing else alongside it.

What it does not print is which part of the fungus that is. The bracket and the mycelium are both sold as reishi powder, and this label does not choose between them.

How to take it

Two capsules a day, with breakfast or lunch, and at least 500 ml of water.

Two is the serving. If you miss a day, take two the next morning rather than four.

What is not printed on the label

Which part of the fungus is in the capsule, an extraction ratio, and where it was grown or gathered. None of the three is on the current label, so none of them is on this page.

The count and the milligrams are printed, which is why there is a cost a day here and none on the chaga.

Storage

Somewhere dry, out of direct sun, with the lid on. A capsule needs no fridge and no cold chain, which is the one practical difference between this and a gel.

Who should not take it

Anyone who reacts to mushrooms should leave it alone. This is a fungus, milled and put into a capsule, and it is an allergen for some people.

If you take medication that thins the blood, ask your GP or pharmacist first. If you are pregnant or nursing, or you take prescribed medication, ask before you start. It is not for children.

Fungus · Ganodermataceae

Ganoderma lucidumReishi, lingzhi

A bracket fungus of dead and dying hardwood, oak and plum among them. The cap is kidney shaped and varnished, red brown over a pale rim, and it grows hard rather than fleshy. Nobody eats it off a plate. Lingzhi is the Chinese name and reishi the Japanese one, and both are a good deal older than the jar.

Reiss's turn

The line I cannot print yet

I sell a lion's mane that says fruiting body on the label, and I have written a whole article about why that phrase is worth the trouble. Then there is this jar, which says reishi, 100 per cent, 800 mg, and stops. It does not say which part of the fungus that is.

I could guess. Reishi has been dried and milled as the bracket for a very long time, and that is what most of the trade means by the word. But a guess in a specification is a lie with good manners, so the sheet above carries a blank instead, and the blank stays until the label answers it.

Everything else here is printed and can be checked: the species, the count, the milligrams, the serving, the price. One line is missing. I would rather you knew which line than found out later that nobody had told you.
