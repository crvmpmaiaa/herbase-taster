Herb · Fabaceae

Astragalus membranaceusAstragalus

A perennial legume with pinnate leaves and pale pea flowers, from northern China, Mongolia and Korea. Astragalus is one of the largest genera of flowering plants, several thousand species wide, which is why the second half of the name does the work here.

From Reiss

Why the Latin matters more on this jar than most

Astragalus is one of the largest genera of flowering plants there is. Several thousand species carry the name, they grow on every continent except Antarctica, and some of them are known to poison grazing stock. The word astragalus on its own tells you almost nothing. Astragalus membranaceus tells you which plant.

That is the whole argument for printing a binomial, and it is why the row above gives the name the listing prints and then adds that some catalogues file it under mongholicus. Two names, one plant, and you should be able to see both from the sheet rather than find out later.

The listing this jar came from carries a stack of study links. There are no study links on this page. What I can give you is the species, 800 mg a capsule, 50 capsules, two a day with lunch, and the fact that nobody here has yet written down which part of the plant was milled.
