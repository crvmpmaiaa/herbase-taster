What is in the box

One jar of Mag Flux, 50 large vegan capsules. One jar of Decolonise, 50 vegan capsules at 800 mg. One jar of the Purple Mwani gel, which is Eucheuma cottonii and water.

Nothing else is named as being in the box. The three cards below go to each jar's own sheet.

The schedule as printed on the listing

Morning: one tablespoon of the sea moss gel. Afternoon: two Mag Flux capsules. Evening: two Decolonise capsules.

All capsules with at least 500 ml of water, and the listing asks for the schedule to be followed for a full 25 day cycle. That is the listing's own wording and this page does not add to it.

The stop date

Decolonise is 25 days and then at least four months before another course, which is printed on its own sheet and does not change because the jar came in a box.

Activated charcoal is one of its nine declared ingredients. Charcoal can reduce how much of a medicine taken by mouth the body takes up, including the contraceptive pill, so leave at least two hours between the capsules and anything you take on prescription.

Who should not take it

Not in pregnancy or while breastfeeding, and not if you are trying to conceive: that last one is the Purple Mwani listing's own caution and we print it as it stands. Not for children.

Anyone with epilepsy or a history of seizures should leave the Decolonise jar alone. Anyone with an overactive thyroid should leave the sea moss alone, and if you take thyroid medication or anything for the blood, ask your GP or pharmacist first. Yellow dock, in Mag Flux, is high in oxalate, so ask first if you have been told to keep oxalate down.

Reiss's turn

The jar in the middle has a stop date

Decolonise is 25 days and then four months on the shelf, and that limit does not soften because the jar arrived in a box with two others. It is on the sheet above in its own row for that reason. If somebody buys this in March, the earliest I would want them opening another Decolonise is July.

The second thing on that jar is the charcoal. Activated charcoal is one of the nine declared ingredients and charcoal is not selective about what it holds on to. If you take anything on prescription, including the pill, put two hours between it and the evening capsules. That is not caution for the sake of it, it is how charcoal behaves.

On the money: buy the three separately at the prices on this site and it comes to £104.97, so the box saves you £4.98. That is the whole of it. I have left the listing's own saving figure off this page because it does not agree with the shelf prices, and I would rather print the arithmetic you can check than the number that reads better.

The earliest I would want you opening another Decolonise is four months after you close this one.
