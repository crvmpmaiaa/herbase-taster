From Reiss

What is not in it is the easy half

No fillers, no preservatives. I stand behind that sentence, it is checkable, and any manufacturer can be held to it. It is also the easy half of a label. The hard half is the list of what is actually in the capsule, in Latin, with the part of the plant named, and that half is missing from this listing.

I have left the ingredient frames on this page empty for that reason. Filling them with a plausible plant would have made the page look finished, and it would have been an invention. The people who most need the list are the ones on a prescription, and they need the real one or nothing.

So the sheet prints the serving, the price, the capsule and the two absences, and stops. If you want to know what is in the jar before you buy it, ring the shop and somebody will go and read the sack to you. When the list is printed on the label it goes on this page in full.

A jar can tell you truthfully what is not in it and still not tell you what is.
