From Reiss

Three badges are not a list

Vegan, non-GMO, wildcrafted. I have printed all three on this page because the listing prints them and they are checkable, and I have then had to write the same sentence three times: none of them tells you what is in the jar. A capsule can be vegan and contain nine plants I have not named, and it would still carry every badge.

The reason a list matters is dull and practical. Somebody comes into the shop on four prescriptions and asks whether this one is all right alongside them. With a list I can read it out and they can take it to a pharmacist. Without one, the only honest answer is that nobody can tell, and I would rather say that on the page than at the till after the money has changed hands.

So the sheet above prints what exists and leaves the rest blank. If the label prints a list, it goes on here in full, in Latin, with the part of each plant named, in the order it is declared. That is what the Decolonise sheet looks like and it is what this one should look like.

A capsule can carry every badge on the shelf and still not tell you what is in it.
