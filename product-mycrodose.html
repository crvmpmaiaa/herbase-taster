What is on the inside

Four mushrooms, one capsule

The listing names four fungi and the shell, and prints 100 per cent pure against each of the four. What it does not print is how the 800 mg is divided between them.

Fungus · Hericiaceae

Hericium erinaceusLion's mane

The white spined mass that grows out of old hardwood, usually beech or oak. It has no cap and no gills. We sell it on its own as well, and that label names the fruiting body. This one does not say which part is in the blend.

Fungus · Cordycipitaceae

Cordyceps militarisScarlet caterpillar club

A small orange club fungus that fruits from buried insect pupae, and the one of the four that is usually farmed on grain or rice rather than gathered off wood.

Fungus · Hymenochaetaceae

Inonotus obliquusChaga

The hard black mass on a living birch, cracked like charcoal outside and rust orange inside. On its own it is 750 mg a capsule. Here it is one of four and its share of the 800 mg is not printed.

Fungus · Ganodermataceae

Ganoderma lucidumReishi, lingzhi

The varnished, kidney shaped bracket of dead and dying hardwood, hard rather than fleshy. The single jar is 50 at 800 mg, and that label does not name a part either.

The shell · Named

Hydroxypropyl methylcelluloseThe vegan capsule

This is the only jar on the shelf whose listing names the shell material. Hydroxypropyl methylcellulose is made from plant cellulose, and it is what vegan capsule usually means when a label bothers to say. The capsules in the picture are a generated scene rather than a photograph of ours.
