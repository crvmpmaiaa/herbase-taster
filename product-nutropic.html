From Reiss

Why this page is mostly blank

A jar that does not print its ingredients is a jar nobody can check. Not your GP, not the pharmacist reading your repeat prescription, and not me at the counter with the tub in my hand. That is the whole of my objection to it, and it is why the sheet above says not printed in row after row instead of filling the gaps with something plausible.

The word on the front is a name. It is not a description and it is not a promise, and this page does not translate it into one. If you want to know what you are swallowing, the ingredient list is the only thing that answers that question, and it is not printed yet.

If the list gets printed, it goes here in full: every plant in Latin, the part of it used, in the order the label declares them, the way the Decolonise sheet does it. Until then the page stays thin and the tub stays on the shelf for people who already know what they are buying and have asked.

A jar that will not print its ingredients is a jar nobody can check.
