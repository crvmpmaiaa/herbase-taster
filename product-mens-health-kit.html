What is in the box

One 500 ml jar of the Chondrus Crispus gel. One jar of Mag Flux, 50 large vegan capsules at 800 mg. One jar of Test Drive, 50 large vegan capsules at 800 mg.

The listing also says every order comes with the Konscious Konsumption Nutrition Guide. It is not sold on its own and carries no price, so it is not in the sold separately figure below.

The schedule as printed on the listing

Morning: one tablespoon of the gel. Lunch: one or two Mag Flux capsules. An hour before exercise or sport: one or two Test Drive capsules. The listing names a third occasion which we are not able to reprint on a shop page, because printing it would say what the jar is for.

Always with at least 500 ml of spring water, as printed. The Test Drive sheet prints a different serving again, two capsules with breakfast and at least 1 litre of spring water across the day, and both are set out on the sheet below.

Storage

The gel goes in the fridge, used within 28 days of opening or frozen, with a clean dry spoon. Capsules keep their lid on somewhere cool and dry, no fridge.

Who should not take it

Not for children. Not in pregnancy or while breastfeeding. The Test Drive listing states that jar is for men only; it gives no reason, and we are not going to invent one.

If you take nitrates, blood pressure medicine, heart medicine or any other prescribed medicine, speak to your GP or pharmacist before you start. Piperine, in Test Drive, is well known to change how the body takes up and clears some medicines. Pine pollen is a pollen, so anyone who reacts to pollen should leave that jar alone. Anyone with an overactive thyroid should leave the sea moss alone, and yellow dock, in Mag Flux, is high in oxalate.
