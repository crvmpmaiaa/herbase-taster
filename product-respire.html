From Reiss

One name out of an unknown number

Proprietary blend is a phrase that tells you there is more in the jar and does not tell you what. I have used it myself in the past and I am not going to pretend the words are neutral: the effect of them is that a customer, a pharmacist and a shopkeeper are all left with the same information, which is none.

The one herb this listing does name, it names in Spanish. Amor seco is a name several plants answer to, and the two you meet most often in the trade are not even in the same family. Print a binomial and you are telling somebody which plant they are swallowing. I am not prepared to tell them that until the label says it, so the frame on the card above is empty and the sheet says the same.

What would fix this page is short and dull: the species behind the Spanish name, the part of the plant, the rest of the blend itemised, and the milligrams. Four lines. Until they are printed I would rather this page reads thin than reads finished.
