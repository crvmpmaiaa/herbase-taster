The open questions

Building these pages meant reading every listing for facts rather than adjectives, and the reading turned up 148 things the shop does not currently print anywhere. None of them have been guessed at. They are all here, by product, so they can be answered once and then appear on the pages for good. This page is a working document and would not ship on the live site.

  • What proportion of the 800 mg capsule is clove versus the four algae? Not stated in the listing.
  • Confirm whether the Chondrus crispus used in Seaking is wild harvested or farmed.
