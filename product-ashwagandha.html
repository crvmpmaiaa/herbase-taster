What is in it

Withania somnifera, milled, in a vegan capsule, with no filler or bulking agent named.

The listing calls the contents a pure herb in one line and a pure extract in another. Those are different materials and only one can be in the capsule, so the sheet below says the question is not resolved.

How to take it

Two capsules in the morning, with at least 500 ml of water.

If you miss a morning, take two the next morning rather than four.

What is not printed on the label

The count, the milligrams, the part of the plant, the extraction ratio and the origin.

The variant line reads 100 g and does not say what is being weighed, so it is not used as a strength anywhere on this page. With no count there is no day rate either.

Storage

Dry, out of direct sun, lid on. It does not need a fridge.

Who should not take it

Not for children, and not in pregnancy or breastfeeding. This is a plant of the nightshade family, so leave it alone if you avoid those.

Reports of liver injury have been recorded in people taking ashwagandha products. If you have a liver condition, or you take prescribed medication of any kind, particularly for the thyroid, ask your GP or pharmacist before you start.

Shrub · Solanaceae

Withania somniferaAshwagandha

A small grey green shrub of dry ground, grown across India and into the Middle East and parts of Africa. It sits in the nightshade family, alongside tomato, potato and physalis, and its red berry is held in the same sort of papery husk.

From Reiss

Powder or extract, and why the sheet says neither

The listing for this jar manages to say two things at once. In one line the contents are 100 per cent pure herb. In another they are a pure extract. A milled root and an extract of a milled root are different materials, made differently, and sold at different strengths. Only one of them is in the capsule.

If it is an extract there should be a ratio, five to one or ten to one or whatever it is, and there is not one. I am not going to pick the answer that sounds better, print it, and hope. The sheet above says not resolved in that row, which is an ugly thing to have on your own product page and a true one.
