What is in it

Hericium erinaceus, the fruiting body, dried and milled into a vegan capsule. There is one ingredient and there is no second one.

The fruiting body is the white spined mass that grows out of the tree. It is not mycelium, and it is not mycelium grown on grain, so there is no rice flour or oat starch milled in with it.

How to take it

Two capsules in the morning, with at least 500 ml of water.

Two is the serving. If you miss a morning, take two the next morning rather than four.

Storage

Somewhere dry, out of direct sun, with the lid on. A capsule needs no fridge and no cold chain, which is the one practical difference between this and the gel.

Who should not take it

Anyone who reacts to mushrooms should leave it alone. This is a mushroom, milled and put in a capsule, and it is an allergen for some people.

If you are pregnant or nursing, or you take prescribed medication, ask your GP or pharmacist before you start. It is not for children.

Fungus · Hericiaceae

Hericium erinaceusLion's mane

A white fungus of old hardwood, usually beech or oak. It has no cap and no gills. It fruits as one mass of hanging spines, which is where every one of its common names comes from. Here it is growing straight out of the bark.

The part used · Fruiting body

The fruiting bodyWhat goes in the capsule

The spined mass itself, the part that grows out of the bark. Mycelium is the other part, the thread network inside the wood. Both are the same organism, and only one of them is in here.

From Reiss

Why the label says fruiting body

A lion's mane has two parts worth naming. The mycelium is the thread network that lives inside the wood and does the growing. The fruiting body is the white spined mass that comes out of the bark once the fungus is ready, and it is the part people have been eating for a very long time.

Growing a fruiting body takes months and a lot of room. Growing mycelium is quicker: you sterilise a bag of grain, usually brown rice or oats, and let the threads run through it. What comes out is a block of grain with fungus in it. Dry that block, mill it, and the grain goes into the capsule with everything else, because nobody has taken it out. That is a real product and it is legal to sell, but it is not what this jar is.

So the label says fruiting body, and that is the whole of what I am claiming. It does not tell you what it will do for you, because I am not allowed to say that and I would not guess it. It tells you which part of which fungus you have bought.

If a label will not tell you which part of the fungus is in it, that is the answer.
