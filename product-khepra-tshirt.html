What it is

A black t-shirt in a cotton blend, cut unisex, with a solar scarab printed on the front. The listing ties the design to Khepra, the scarab figure of Egyptian myth, which is where the name comes from.

That description of the graphic is the listing's own. There is no photograph on this page for you to check it against, which is the honest position until we have one.

Sizing

Not printed. The listing carries a single option with no size on it, no size chart and no garment measurements.

Ring the shop before you order. Somebody will lay one flat and read you the chest and the length off a tape, which is worth two minutes on a shirt at this price.

What is not printed

The fibre percentages in the blend, the fabric weight in grams per square metre, the printing method, the blank the shirt is printed on, and the country it was made in.

Every one of those is a row on the sheet below that says not printed. None of them have been guessed at.

Care and delivery

Machine wash cold and hang to dry, as printed on the listing.

Sent out from 599 Smithdown Road, Liverpool, Tuesday to Sunday, or collect from the shop between 10 and 6, any day except Monday.
