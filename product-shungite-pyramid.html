What it is

Shungite is a black rock with a high carbon content. The commercial stone is quarried in Karelia, in north west Russia, and takes its name from the village of Shunga. That is a fact about the mineral rather than about this piece: the listing does not say where this one came from.

The piece is worked into a pyramid, 8 cm by 8 cm. The listing does not say whether that is base by height or two sides of the base.

What is printed

One measurement, 8 cm by 8 cm, and a price. Everything else on the sheet below says not printed.

No weight, no finish, no grade word, no origin for this piece, no assay and no certificate.

What this page does not say

The listing this pyramid came from makes a number of statements about what it does and where to put it. None of them are repeated here, and none of them are made here in different words either.

The listing also carries the sentence that the product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease. That sentence is an American one. It does not make the paragraphs above it lawful in Britain, so those paragraphs are not on this page.
