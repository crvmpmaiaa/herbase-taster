What is in it

Shilajit resin from the Altai Mountains, dual-extracted, and nothing else. There is no second ingredient to name.

The jar holds 50 g. The listing puts that at about two months at one portion a day.

How to take it

A pea-sized portion, once a day, in the morning. Stir it into 300 to 500 ml of warm water until none of it is left on the spoon or the bottom of the glass.

Warm water, not boiling. One portion a day is the serving. If you missed yesterday, do not take two today.

What lab tested means here

Shilajit is collected out of mountain rock, and the test that matters on it is a heavy-metal test: lead, arsenic, mercury and cadmium.

We do not publish the certificate on this site. So read the words lab tested as a statement that the testing is done, not as a result you have read for yourself. Ring the shop and ask for the analysis for the batch you are buying. Ask anybody else selling you a resin for the same thing.

What we do not print

No mineral list. Shilajit pages tend to carry a list of mineral names with no amounts against any of them. A list without amounts is not a specification, which is why this section does not carry one.

No weight for the portion. A pea is not a unit. Nobody weighs what comes out of the jar, so there is no figure in grams or milligrams for a serving; printing one anyway would only make the page look more precise than it is.

No photograph of the jar. The pack shot we hold carries wording we are not willing to publish, so it stays off the site until the label is reprinted. The mountains above are the range it is collected from; the spoon and glass are a generated scene, not our jar.

Who should not take it

Not for children. If you are pregnant or nursing, or you take prescribed medication, ask your GP or pharmacist before you start.

Do not take raw resin bought loose or picked up on a trip. Purifying it and testing it for heavy metals is most of what you are paying for in a jar.

The routine

How to take it

Step one

Pour the water first. 300 to 500 ml, warm enough to dissolve a resin and not so hot you cannot drink it.

Step two

Take a pea-sized portion. That is the serving on the jar. It is not a weighed dose, and we do not print one.

Step three

Stir until it has gone. Nothing left on the spoon, nothing sitting at the bottom of the glass. Drink it in the morning.

Step four

Lid back on. Once a day is the serving. If you missed yesterday, do not take two today.

From Reiss

Why there is no mineral list on this page

Every shilajit page I have read gives you a list of mineral names as long as your arm, and not one number against any of them. I could copy a list like that onto this page in five minutes and it would look impressive. It would also tell you nothing about what is in this particular jar, because a list of names is not a measurement, and printing the names without a measurement behind them would only decorate the page.

So the sheet above has gaps in it, and I have labelled the gaps. What I can give you is the range it came from, what the label says about how it was prepared, what the testing is for, and how much goes in the glass. What I cannot give you is a figure per portion, or a certificate you can read on this page, or an account of what happens after you drink it. If any of that is what you came for, ring me before you buy rather than after.

A list of names is not a measurement.
