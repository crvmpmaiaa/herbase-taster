Book the hourOne hour · £120.00

Not sure it is the right thing to buy? Ring the shop on 07946 806533, Tuesday to Sunday, and ask before you book. Some questions take two minutes at the counter and cost nothing.

What happens

You book it here. An email follows with instructions for choosing a time that suits you. At the time you chose, the call happens, and it runs for an hour.

Bring your questions written down. An hour goes quickly, and most people spend the first ten minutes remembering what they meant to ask.

What it is not

It is not a medical appointment. Nothing said in the hour is a diagnosis, a prescription, or a reason to stop or change anything you have been prescribed.

If you take prescribed medication, your GP and your pharmacist are the two people who can check anything against it. If what you need is urgent, ring 111 or your surgery rather than booking an hour.

What is not printed

Whether the call is recorded, whether you are sent notes afterwards, what happens if you need to move the time, how far ahead the diary runs, and what the letters after the name stand for.

None of that is on the listing, so none of it is promised here. Ring the shop and ask before you book if any of it decides the matter for you.

Where it happens

On a private audio call, not in the shop. The listing does not name the platform it is made on or say who rings whom.

If you would rather ask at a counter, the shop is at 599 Smithdown Road, Liverpool, open 10 to 6, any day except Monday.
