# Reiss, the questions to send by message

Written to be answered by voice note. Send one batch at a time, three or four lines each, a day apart. Each line says in brackets what it unlocks. If he passes on one, the fallback is always the narrower, honest version, so nothing here blocks anything for long.

Batch order matters: 1 and 2 gate the plan, 3 feeds the content engine, the rest can land in any order.

---

## Batch 1: the two numbers, and the gel

1. What does one £39.99 jar of capsules cost you to get on the shelf, all in, ingredients, capsules, label, packing? [the margin; gates the trade price, the kit prices and the whole plan]
2. What does the business pay out in a normal month before you buy any stock: rent, rates, wages, subscriptions, insurance, the lot? [the same]
3. The sea moss gel: are you going for the Environmental Health certificate on the current kitchen, or moving production to a registered unit, and roughly when? [when gel and the kits come back online; until then everything runs on capsules]
4. Is Herbase a limited company or are you trading as a sole trader, and what is the exact legal name? [the footer the law asks for, and it decides which Pay by Bank provider we can use]

## Batch 2: the two things that unlock the email flows

5. The paid hour: what happens in it, what should it cost, and how many hours a week are you willing to give it? [the consultation flow; seven email flows are waiting on this and the next one]
6. Would you offer a free ten-minute call as the first step, and could someone other than you take it? [same]
7. Wholesale: would you supply shops, gyms, yoga studios or practitioners, and if so what is the least you would want an order to be? [the trade sheet; needs the answer to question 1]

## Batch 3: your customers, in your words

8. The ten questions you get asked most, at the till and in your messages. Just say them as they come, in the words people use. [this is the fuel for every article, letter and reel, the single most useful voice note on this list]
9. Who buys from you: age, where they live, how they found you, how many travel in, how many are students? [who the emails are written to]
10. The 21-day line and the 25-day supply: how do you explain that to someone at the counter? [the reorder and check-in emails, said your way]
11. Which products are taken every day for as long as you like, and which are a course with a break, and how long is the break? [the stop dates on the pages; only four are printed anywhere]

## Batch 4: the product facts

12. Northern Soul: is the current jar the four algae on the listing, or the six items in the book? [the formula page]
13. Purple Mwani: cultivated off Tanzania, or wildcrafted from Zanzibar? [the listing]
14. How many Detox Redox capsules a day, and with which meal? [nothing is printed]
15. Are the powders micronised already, or is that something you would want done? [a spec to publish, or a product change]
16. The ones that contradict themselves, one answer each: Ginseng, Siberian or Panax? Feminina, which of the two ingredient lists? Anamu, how many days does a pack last? Equill, one serving or two?

## Batch 5: evidence and safety

17. Do you hold any lab sheet or supplier certificate with a measured figure on it, for anything other than the iodine in Seaking? [every claim the register would let us make]
18. The ten safety cautions on the new site: we will send them as a list, sign off, change or strike each. [the caution lines]
19. Which of the Instagram testimonials are real customers who would be happy to be quoted? [none is published until this list exists]
20. Is the Trustpilot page yours, and who has the login? [one 3.5 star review sits next to your 4.8 on Google for anyone who searches]

## Batch 6: the shop, the people, the paperwork

21. Who else works in the business, who minds the shop when you are filming, and who would pack parcels if orders went to sixty a day? [the plan assumes about 1,800 parcels a month at the target]
22. Opening hours, and the number people can ring. [the "is the shop open" answer]
23. Are you VAT registered? [what every price nets to]
24. What do you charge for postage, and what does a parcel cost you to send? Would you ship outside the UK? [the delivery line]
25. Is "Herbase" registered as a trade mark, and if so when? [the wordmark redraw]
26. Have you paid the ICO data protection fee, and do you hold product liability insurance, and with whom? [clears the first email send; complaint cover]

## Batch 7: camera, community, creators

27. The documentary: what is it, who is making it, when does it land, and can we use clips? [whether it is mentioned at all]
28. The monthly live hour: would you do one, and is it your room or the brand's? [the community flow]
29. Affiliates and creators: who do you already know, and what terms would you be happy with? [the creator list, your half of it]
30. Filming in the shop: we will draft a one-line consent form for customers who appear; are you happy to use it? [rights]
31. Marketplaces: Amazon, TikTok Shop, any of them ever, or never? [one line, closes the question]

## Batch 8: the books and the money rules

32. How much of the books are you happy to give away in emails and articles, and would you give the first book away free for an email address? [the free book offer]
33. The 44-day cohort: would you run it as a paid group, and what would you want it to cost? [the top rung]
34. What would you spend a month on marketing right now, and what would you never spend on? [budget posture]
35. Do you count two books published, or three? [the author box]
