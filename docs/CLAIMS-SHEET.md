# Permitted claims sheet, 22 September 2026

The line we can print for each product, word for word from the government lists. Green is safe as written, teal is usable with some exposure, black has no lawful line. Red words have to come off the pack. Footnotes. On-hold claims have no published conditions of use; they may be used only while they stay on hold, with evidence held for the preparation and dose, and never as a medicinal claim. The on-hold route rests on Article 28(5) and (6) of the retained Regulation, which were omitted for Great Britain on 31 December 2020 by SI 2019/651; the Department of Health still publishes the list and Trading Standards still honour it. Settled practice on unsettled law.
