# VOICE.md: how Reiss Davies Ausar writes

## Read this first

The corpus writes; this guide is the editor's checklist. Three to six of his passages are the spine of a piece and our sentences only join them. His text is exempt from every rule here. Ours is not.

Five tells, all five from rounds 1 to 3: a balanced antithesis or a "not X, it is Y" of our making (R31, R120); a caps epigram or two beat aphorism we wrote rather than quoted (R28, R122); an explained coinage (R66, R125); a clever subject line (R93, R124); an em-dash or en-dash (R70).

His formats, each beside a verbatim example, plus the email skeleton and word bands: section 12. How he reasons: section 11. His wit: section 13. Every human verdict, which outranks the rest of this file: section 14. The registers: section 1. The numbers: section 2. Email furniture: section 10. DR pages run one above the printed folio, as in the index; NF pages equal it.
