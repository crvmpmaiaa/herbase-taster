# LEXICON.md: his words, his spellings, our house style

## Read this first

**Table 1** is every deliberate coinage and respelling in the corpus, with its meaning, count and citations. Use these; never invent a new one. His rate is about two per 1,000 words (1.9 in the 2025 book, 2.5 in the 2019 one), so a 400 word email gets one, occasionally two. **Table 2** is the candidate slips: things that look like typing, not craft. Quote them verbatim inside a quotation, never reproduce them in new copy, until Reiss rules. **Section 3** is our house style and binds every draft.

The `Reiss` column in both tables is blank on purpose. It goes to him with the six calibration emails (`ASK-REISS.md`, "Reiss's twenty minutes"); he marks each `yes`, `no` or `retired`. Counts are occurrences across `corpus/books/detox-redox-2025.txt` and `corpus/books/nu-flow-the-ripple-effect-2019.txt` unless the row cites the site.

## 3. House style

This section is ours, not his, and it binds every draft. It is enforced by `node tools/claims-check.mjs`.

**Spelling.** British throughout: colour, realise, litre, metre, fibre. American spellings only inside a verbatim quotation.

**Dashes.** The spaced hyphen is the only dash: `Buckminster Fuller - Integrity is the essence of everything successful`. No em-dash, no en-dash, anywhere, including numeric ranges: write `9am to 11am` and `1 to 2 tablespoons`, not the dashed forms in the live listings. Evidence: zero em-dashes in Nu Flow and five in Detox Redox, all five on the numerology and tuning pages 20 to 22 (DR p.20 L771, p.20 L779-787, p.22 L848-858), which the passage index reads as his own writing rather than quoted material; on the site, 40 across 18 of 48 listing files, concentrated in template copy. Five in 56,000 words is not a habit, so the ban stands either way (`VOICE.md` R70).

**Prices.** `£24.99`, never `24.99 GBP`, never a rounded "from" price. He prices nothing in either book, so a price appears only in a product block, never in prose.

**Units.** A thin space between figure and unit: `800 mg`, `75 µg`, `500 ml`, `50 g`. Capsule counts as digits. A dose or a unit is never softened. A count, an age or an inventory may be, because his are: "over 20+ different herbs" (SITE listings/detox-redox.txt L13), "over 80 minerals" (SITE listings/shilajit-resin.txt L14), "approx. 2 month supply" (SITE listings/shilajit-resin.txt L12). See `VOICE.md` R49 and R74.

**The ampersand.** His conjunction inside a banner, a caption or a clock range, 69 times in Detox Redox and 67 in Nu Flow: `between 10am & 12pm`, `ME YOU & THE COMMUNITY`. In running prose write "and".

**Apostrophes.** He drops them constantly (thats, dont, wont, Im, its for it's). House decision: keep them in everything we write, and keep his verbatim inside a quotation. The absence of the dropped apostrophe is not a tell a judge can hold against us, because we are writing marketing copy and he was typing a book; see section 2 and `VOICE.md` R78.

**Product names.** Every product name is a proper noun, set as written: Prostrate, Nutropic, RESPiRE, Mag Flux, Seaking, Test Drive, Decolonise, Nu Heru, Northern Soul, Mycrodose, Pancrea, Feminina, Neptune, Equill, Khepra, Galaxeye. A name that implies a condition is never explained, glossed or justified. His own pun line exists already and is his to use, not ours to repeat next to a buy link.

**Capitals.** For a banner line or a single stressed word, never a paragraph of body copy and never a product benefit.

**Links, merge fields and dates.** A link in a body is a plain address, `herbase.earth/books` or `www.herbase.earth`, lower case in prose and in capitals inside a caps banner (`VOICE.md` R37, R90). A brace token never appears in a body: `{{...}}` merge fields are confined to the footer line, and a date is written out as a date, worked out for the reader rather than left as arithmetic or a placeholder (`VOICE.md` R53, R90). In round 1 of the blind test every judge who met an unfilled placeholder named it as the giveaway.

**The words the house bans**, not the words he avoids: the `banned` list in `tools/claims-words.json` is elevate, unlock, journey, delve, seamless, harness, ultimate, premium, game-changer, supercharge, "is not just", "more than a", and two of them are his. "journey" is his five times in the books (DR p.9 L302, DR p.15 L570, DR p.18 L683; NF p.7 L146; NF p.77 L1709) and on the homepage ("Our journey into natural sciences", SITE pages-prose/index.txt L10), and "elevate your energy" is the homepage's own line (SITE pages-prose/index.txt L8). We still do not write them: they read as AI in 2026 marketing copy, which is what the ban is for. Quote them where he wrote them; never reach for them yourself.

Plus the template hand's openers and triplets, which are nobody's voice: "Master the art of", "Designed by herbal specialists", "adapt, restore and thrive", "Why Choose", "Why You'll Love It", "The Herbase Standard".

Plus the hedges he does not write: "may support", "studies suggest", "is thought to". "can help" and "inspired by" are struck from this list: "can help" is his four times in Detox Redox (DR p.5 L157, DR p.59 L2156, DR p.59 L2186) and "inspired by" is his on a `site-his` listing (SITE listings/nuheruseamoss.txt L41). The hedge that is never his is the one on an effect (`VOICE.md` R69).

**Claims.** Nothing here authorises a claim. What a product may be said to do is `COMPLIANCE.md`; a claim in his own words is still a claim.
